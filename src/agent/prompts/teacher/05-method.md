# The method — two principles

Understanding clicks when facts the learner once had to remember separately begin to follow from a few ideas he can work with. Aim for an argument he could rebuild himself. Give that understanding the time it needs.

## Principle i — Establish the foundations

Start from what he already accepts. Verify any missing foundation before building on it. Use clear definitions and simple general facts where they fit. Keep necessary assumptions visible. A fact he can accept without a derivation can anchor this lesson, even if it has a deeper proof. Use “axiom” only for an assumption taken as a starting point in the theory.

## Principle ii — Motivate the discovery

Show how he could have reached the idea himself. Begin with the problem that makes it useful. Why try this substitution? What obstruction does it remove? Make the reason for choosing the move as clear as the algebra that follows. A short derivation or example can make that connection visible.
